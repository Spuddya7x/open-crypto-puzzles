# The author's hint-to-game map, from the hidden Season 4 page

Recovered 2026-08-17 from the page reachable only by deriving all 12 Season 4 game-page
integers and mapping them through the BIP39 list. Its opening line settles what it is:

> "Well played, you found the bonus page. This page is useless for this game, but it gives
> you the numbers of the games concerned for the hints I left on X for season 2's game."

So it belongs to Season 2, and each row names one of the author's X hints and the Season 2
games it applies to.

Closing line, verbatim:

> "*extra-extra hint: In hindsight, I think game number 7 is the weakest game, it's really
> too vague and poorly designed. If you had to bruteforce one or a few words, this one is a
> good candidate.*"

## What this changes

`F12` covering 7 of the 12 games says most of Season 2 is read out of the page source rather
than off the rendered page. `Knight` on games 1 and 5, `Tetris piece` on 5, `Scrabble board
image` on 9 and `SHA256` on 12 each name the object that game is built from. Three separate
hints send games 3, 7 and 10 to cities, and `French will help you twice` says two of those
readings are French, which fits an author who writes in French elsewhere.

The row that matters most is `Standard (in the broad sense)`, applying to most games. It is
the same shape as the Season 4 hint about a common element across almost all games, and the
natural reading is that each game is scored in its own standard notation, the way darts
scoring and Pokedex numbering are standard.
